# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to uncover trends in revenue, product performance, and customer purchasing behavior.  
The goal is to provide **data-driven insights** that help business stakeholders improve sales, optimize inventory, and increase profitability.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Identify top-selling coffee products and categories
- Analyze sales trends by time (daily, monthly)
- Understand revenue contribution by product and location
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales performance

---

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source:** Coffee sales transactional dataset                                                                                                                                                                                
  
    
  
  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Excel (data cleaning, pivot tables, basic analysis)  
- SQL   
- Power BI                      
  

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and validation (handling missing values, formatting dates)
- Revenue and sales volume analysis
- Product-level performance analysis
- Time-based trend analysis (daily)                                                              
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- A small number of coffee products contribute the majority of total revenue
- Sales peak during specific time periods (e.g., mornings or weekends)
- Certain locations consistently outperform others in revenue
- Seasonal trends significantly impact demand for specific products

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus promotions on top-performing products to maximize revenue
- Introduce targeted discounts during low-sales periods
- Optimize inventory planning based on seasonal demand patterns
- Expand or replicate strategies from high-performing locations
